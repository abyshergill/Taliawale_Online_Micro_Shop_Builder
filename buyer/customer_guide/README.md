## Customer Guide

This application lets you browse stores, place orders, and track your deliveries — all in a few simple steps.

---

### 1. Create Your Account

1. Click **Sign Up** in the top menu bar.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Full Name** — Your name
   - **Email** — Your email address (this is also used for login)
   - **Mobile Number** — Your phone number
   - **Password** — Choose a strong password (at least 9 characters, not entirely numeric, not a common password)
   - **Password Confirmation** — Re-enter the same password
3. Click **Create Account**.

You'll be automatically logged in and taken to your profile page.

> **Note:** You can also place orders without an account (as a guest), but you'll need to enter your details each time. With an account, your information is saved for faster checkout.

---

### 2. Log In

1. Click **Login** in the top menu bar.
2. Enter your **username or email address** in the username field.
3. Enter your **password**.
4. Click **Log In**.

> You can log in with either your username or your email address — both work.

---

### 3. Browse Stores

1. Click **Browse Stores** in the top menu.
2. Use the filter bar to find stores:

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Search by Name** | Type a store name to search |
| **Store Category** | Filter by store type (Food, Electronics, Fashion, etc.) |
| **Pincode** | Find stores in your area by pincode |
| **Search by Item Name** | Find stores that sell a specific item (e.g. "Pizza", "Burger") |

3. Use one filter or combine multiple filters together for better results.
4. Click **Clear Filters** to reset.

Each store card shows:
- Store name and category badge
- Address and pincode
- Open now / Closed status
- Matching items (when searching by item name)

Click any store card to open the store page.

---

### 4. Shop Inside a Store

#### Browse the Menu

- Items are organized by **categories** (e.g. Drinks, Main Course, Desserts).
- Each category shows **3 items** by default — click **See all** to view the rest.
- Click a category name in the **top menubar** to jump to that section.
- Categories can be **collapsed/expanded** by clicking the category header.

#### Search Items

- Use the **Search Items** bar to find specific items within the store.
- Click**Clear** to reset the search and see all items again.

#### Add Items to Cart

1. For each item, use the **−** and **+** buttons to set the quantity.
2. Click **Add to Cart**.
3. A green toast notification confirms the item was added.
4. The **sticky bar at the bottom** shows your cart count and a **Go to Checkout** button.
5. Continue adding more items or proceed to checkout.

> If an item shows **Out of stock**, it cannot be ordered.

#### Share the Store

- Click **Share Store** to share the store link via WhatsApp, email, SMS, or any sharing app on your phone.
- Click **Download QR** to save the store's QRcode as an image.

---

### 5. Checkout & Place Order

1. Click **Go to Checkout** at the bottom of the store page.
2. Review your cart:
   - Each item shows the quantity, unit price, tax, and line total
   - The summary shows **Subtotal**, **Tax**, and **Total (incl. tax)**
3. Fill in your details:

| Field | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Customer Name** | Your name (always required) |
| **Customer Address** | Your delivery address (always required) |
| **Contact Phone** | Your phone number (always required) |
| **Table Number** | Required only if the store owner has enabled it |
| **Contact Email** | Required only if the store owner has enabled it |
| **Remarks** | Special requests or notes for the store (optional) |

4. If you're logged in with an account:
   - Your name, phone, and email are **pre-filled** automatically
   - You can select a **saved address** from the dropdown to auto-fill the address field
5. Click **Place Order**.

> If the store is closed and the owner hasn't enabled "Accept Orders When Closed", you'll see a message and won't be able to place an order.

---

### 6. Order Confirmation

After placing your order, you'll see the confirmation page with:

- **Order Number** — Save this number to track your order later
- **Order Status** — Current status (Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, Completed, Cancelled)
- **Items** — List of items with quantities and prices
- **Total** — Subtotal, tax, and grand total
- **Download Bill** — Click to download a PDF receipt

---

### 7. Track Your Order

You can track your order in two ways:

#### Option A — From Order History (Logged-in customers)

1. Click **My Orders** in the top menu.
2. Find your order and click **View Details**.
3. See the current status, items, and total.

#### Option B — Track Order page (Guest or logged-in)

1. Click **Track Order** in the top menu.
2. Enter your **Order Number** (e.g. ORD-ABC123).
3. Enter one piece of verification info:
   - The **phone number** you used at checkout, OR
   - The **email address** you used at checkout, OR
   - The **table number** you used at checkout
4. Click **Track** to see your order status.

> This verification step ensures only you can view your order — a stranger can't look up your order by guessing the number.

---

### 8. View Your Order History

1. Click **My Orders** in the top menu (only visible when logged in).
2. See all your past orders as cards, each showing:
   - Order number and store name
   - Date and time
   - Status badge (color-coded):
     - 🟢 Green = Completed
     - 🔴 Red = Cancelled
     - 🟡 Yellow = Pending
     - 🔵 Blue = Confirmed / Preparing
   - Total amount
   - List of items ordered
3. Click **View Details** to see the full order confirmation page.
4. Click **Download Bill** to get the PDF receipt.

---

### 9. Manage Your Profile

Click **My Profile** in the top menu to manage your account.

#### Update Personal Information

- Edit your **Full Name** and **Mobile Number**.
- Click **Save Changes**.

#### Manage Saved Addresses

You can save multiple addresses for faster checkout:

1. Click **+ Add New**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Alias** — Label for the address (Home, Office, Other)
   - **Address** — Full street address
   - **Pincode** — Area pincode
   - **Phone Number**— Phone number for this address
   - **Is Default** — Tick to make this your primary address
3. Click **Add Address**.

To edit or delete an address:
- Click **Edit** to update an existing address.
- Click **Delete** to remove an address.

> At checkout, your saved addresses appear in a dropdown — select one to auto-fill the address field instantly.

#### Change Username

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Username** section.
2. Click **Change Username**.
3. Enter your new username.
4. Submit.

> You can log in with either your username or email address.

#### Change Password

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Password** section, OR click **Change Password** in the top menu.
2. Enter your **current password**.
3. Enter your **new password** and **confirm** it.
4. Submit.

> You stay logged in after changing your password — no need to log in again.

---

### 10. Notifications

- The 🔔 bell icon in the top menu shows unread notifications.
- You receive notifications when:
  - The store owner **updates your order status** (e.g. Confirmed, Preparing, Completed)
- Click the bell to view all notifications and mark them as read.

---

### 11. Guest Checkout (Without Account)

If you don't want to create an account:

1. Browse stores and add items to your cart — no login required.
2. At checkout, you'll need to **enter all your details manually** every time:
   - Name, address, phone, email, remarks
3. After placing your order, save the **order number** to track it later via the **Track Order** page.

> **Difference from logged-in customers:**
> | Feature | Guest | Logged-in Customer |
> |--------|-------|-------------------|
> | Pre-filled checkout info | ❌ Enter every time | ✅ Auto-filled |
> | Saved addresses | ❌ Not available | ✅ Multiple saved addresses |
> | Order history | ❌ Must track by order number | ✅ Full history in My Orders |
> | Status notifications | ❌ Must check manually | ✅ Automatic notifications |

---

### 12. Contact Us

If you have a complaint, concern, or question:

1. Click **Contact Us** in the top menu.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Name** — Optional
   - **Email** — Your email address (required)
   - **Phone Number** — Your phone number (required)
   - **Message** — Your complaint or concern (max 1000 characters)
3. Click **Submit**.

You can also find the platform's contact email, phone, and address on the same page.
