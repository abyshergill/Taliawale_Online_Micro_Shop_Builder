# TaliaWale — Your Online Store Platform

Welcome to TaliaWale! This platform helps you create your online store in minutes — or browse and order from stores near you. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## What Is TaliaWale?

TaliaWale is a simple online platform where:

- **Store owners** can create their shop, add products, receive orders, and manage everything from one place.
- **Customers** can browse stores, search for items, place orders, and track their deliveries.

Whether you run a food stall, an electronics shop, a fashion boutique, or any small business — TaliaWale gets you online quickly.

---

## Who Is This For?

| Role | What You Can Do |
|------|----------------|
| **Store Owner** | Create a store, add categories and items, manage orders, view sales analytics, share your store via QR code |
| **Customer** | Browse stores, search by item name, place orders, save addresses, track order status, download bills |
| **Guest (No Account)** | Browse stores, place orders by entering details each time, track orders by order number |

---

## Key Features

### For Store Owners

- Set up your store with name, category, picture, currency, and operating hours
- Organize products into categories with custom display order
- Set individual tax rates per item (or mark price as tax-included)
- Track stock levels with automatic low-stock alerts
- Receive notifications when new orders come in
- Create orders manually for walk-in or phone customers
- Edit orders — add, remove, or update item quantities
- Merge multiple orders into a single ticket
- Download printable PDF bills for any order
- View sales analytics — daily, weekly, or monthly
- Share your store via WhatsApp, email, or QR code
- Choose your country timezone so operating hours match your local time
- Accept orders even when closed (optional)

### For Customers

- Search stores by name, category, pincode, or item name
- Browse store menus organized by category
- Search for specific items within a store
- Add items to cart with quantity stepper controls
- Save multiple delivery addresses (Home, Office, etc.)
- Auto-fill checkout details for faster ordering
- Track order status in real time
- View complete order history
- Download PDF bills for past orders
- Get notified when the store updates your order status
- Place orders without an account (guest checkout)

## Getting Started

### Want to Sell?

If you want to create your own online store:

1. Click **Register Your Stall** in the top menu.
2. Fill in your username, email, phone number, and password.
3. Set up your store — name, category, address, timing, and currency.
4. Add your product categories and items.
5. Start receiving orders!

For detailed step-by-step instructions, see the **[Store Owner Guide](05_owner_guide.md)**.

### Want to Buy?

If you want to browse and order from stores:

1. Click **Sign Up** in the top menu and create an account — or just start browsing without one.
2. Click **Browse Stores** to find stores near you.
3. Filter by name, category, pincode, or item name.
4. Open a store, add items to your cart, and check out.
5. Track your order using the order number.

For detailed step-by-step instructions, see the **[Customer Guide](04_customer_guide.md)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account to order?

No. You can browse and order as a guest. However, with an account you get saved addresses, order history, faster checkout, and automatic notifications.

### Can I log in with my email instead of username?

Yes. You can log in with either your username or your email address.

### What if a store is closed?

If a store is closed, you can still browse the menu but cannot place an order — unless the store owner has enabled "Accept Orders When Closed."

### How do I track my order?

Use the **Track Order** page in the top menu. Enter your order number and one piece of verification info (phone, email, or table number) that you used at checkout.

### How do I download my bill?

On the order confirmation page or in My Orders, click **Download Bill** to get a PDF receipt.

### What if I forget my password?

Contact us through the **Contact Us** page. There is no self-service password reset.

### Can I have multiple delivery addresses?

Yes. Logged-in customers can save multiple addresses (Home, Office, Other) and select one at checkout.

### Can the store owner create orders manually?

Yes. Store owners can create orders for walk-in or phone customers by selecting items and entering customer details themselves.

### Can I change the language?

Yes. Use the flag buttons (🇺🇸 EN | 🇹🇭 TH) in the top menu bar to switch between English and Thai. More languages can be added.

## Need Help?

If you have any questions, complaints, or concerns:

1. Click **Contact Us** in the top menu.
2. Fill in your name (optional), email, phone number, and your message (up to 1000 characters).
3. Click **Submit**.

We aim to respond within 24–48 hours.
