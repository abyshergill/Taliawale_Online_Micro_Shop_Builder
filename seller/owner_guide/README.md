## Store Owner Guide

This application helps you create your online shop in just a few minutes. Follow the steps below to register, set up your store, and start receiving orders.

---

### 1. Register as a Store Owner

1. Click **Register Your Stall** in the top menu bar.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Username** — Enter your preferred username
   - **Email** — Your email address (e.g. `yourname@email.com`)
   - **Mobile Number** — Your phone number
   - **Password** — Choose a strong password
   - **Password Confirmation** — Re-enter the same password
3. Click **Create Owner Account**.

> **Note:** If you forget your password, contact us through the **Contact Us** page — there is no self-service password reset.

---

### 2. Set Up Your Store Information

After logging in:

1. Click **My Store** in the top menu.
2. Click **Edit Store**.
3. Fill in the following:

| Field | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Name** | Your store name |
| **Store Category** | Choose the type (Food, Electronics, Fashion, etc.) |
| **Description** | A short description of your store |
| **Pincode** | Your shop's pincode (required) |
| **Address** | Your complete shop address |
| **Phone Number** | Your shop contact number |
| **Store Picture** | Upload a photo of your store |
| **Currency** | Select your currency from the dropdown. Choose **Custom** first if you want to use your own symbol |

4. Under **Checkout Settings**, toggle which fields customers must fill:
   - **Accept Orders When Closed** — Allow orders even when your store is closed (disabled by default)
   - **Require Table Number** — For restaurants and food courts
   - **Require Email** — Customer must provide email at checkout
   - **Require Customer Name** — Customer must enter their name
   - **Require Customer Address** — Customer must provide their delivery address

5. Click **Save Changes**.

---

### 3. Set Your Operating Hours

1. Click **My Store** → **Hours**.
2. **Select your timezone** (e.g. Bangkok, Tokyo, New York) so the system matches your local time.
3. Set the opening and closing time for each day (Monday to Sunday).
4. Tick **Closed** for any day your store is not open.
5. Click **Save Hours**.

> Your store's open/closed status updates automatically based on these times and your selected timezone.

---

### 4. Create Categories

Categories help organize your items into sections (e.g. Drinks, Main Course, Desserts).

1. Click **My Store** → **Categories**.
2. Click **Add** and fill in:
   - **Name** — Category name (e.g. "Drinks")
   - **Display Order** — Controls the sort position:
     - `0` = appears first
     - `1` = appears second
     - `2` = appears third
     - If all categories are `0`, they sort alphabetically
3. Click **Add** to save.

You can **Edit** or **Delete** existing categories at any time.

> **Note:** Deleting a category does not delete its items — they remain in your item list but without a category assigned.

---

### 5. Add Menu Items

1. Click **My Store** → **Menu Items** → **+ Add Item**.
2. Fill in the form:

| Field | Description |
|------|-------------|
|**Category** | Select which category this item belongs to |
| **Name** | Item name (e.g. "Cheese Burger") |
| **Item Code** | Unique code for this item (e.g. SKU, PLU). Leave blank to auto-generate |
| **Description** | A short description shown to customers |
| **Price** | The price displayed to customers |
| **Tax %** | Tax percentage for this item. Set to 0 if the price is final (tax included) |
| **Stock Quantity** | How many units you currently have |
| **Min Stock Quantity** | You'll get an alert when stock drops below this number (default: 10) |
| **Image** | Upload a product photo |
| **Is Available** | Toggle to show/hide this item from customers |

3. Click **Save Item**.

> **Low Stock Alert:** When stock falls below the minimum quantity, the item row turns red with a ⚠ warning, and you receive a notification.

---

### 6. Manage Orders

Click **My Store** → **Orders** to see your order dashboard.

#### Filter Orders

Use the filter bar to search by:

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Order Number** | Search by order number (e.g. ORD-ABC123) |
| **Customer Name** | Search by customer name |
| **Customer Phone** | Search by phone number |
| **Status** | Filter by Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, Completed, Cancelled |
| **From Date / To Date** | Filter by date range |
| **Min / Max Total** | Filter by order amount |
| **Sort By** | Newest first, oldest first, total high-to-low, total low-to-high |

#### Create a New Order Manually

1. Click **+ Create Order**.
2. Select a **Category**, then select an **Item** from that category.
3. Set the **Quantity**.
4. Click **+ Add Another Item** to add more items if needed.
5. Fill in customer details:
   - Customer Name
   - Customer Address
   - Table Number
   - Contact Phone
   - Contact Email
   - Remarks
6. Set the **Status** (e.g. Pending, Confirmed).
7. Click **Create Order**.

#### View & Edit Order Details

1. Click on an **Order Number** to open the order detail page.
2. From here you can:
   - **Edit Customer Details** — Update name, phone, email, address, remarks, and status
   - **Download Bill** — Generate a PDF receipt
   - **Delete Order** — Permanently remove the order
   - **Update Item Quantity** — Change the quantity of any item in the order
   - **Remove Item** — Delete an item from the order
   - **Add Item** — Add a new item to the order (select category first, then item)

#### Merge Orders

1. Click **Merge Orders**.
2. Select two or more orders to combine into a single master ticket.
3. Confirm the merge.

#### Update Order Status

1. Open any order detail page.
2. Use the **Status** dropdown to change the order status.
3. Click **Set Status**.

> The customer receives a notification each time you update the order status.

---

### 7. View Analytics

Click **My Store** → **Analytics** to view your sales dashboard.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Revenue** | Total sales (excluding cancelled orders) |
| **Total Orders** | Number of active orders |
| **Sales Over Time** | Chart showing daily, weekly, or monthly sales |
| **Top Items** | Best-selling items by revenue |
| **Category Sales** | Revenue breakdown by category |

Use the **Period** dropdown to switch between daily (30 days), weekly (90 days), or monthly (365 days) views.

---

### 8. Share Your Store & QR Code

On your store page:
- **Share Store** — Opens the native share menu (WhatsApp, email, SMS, etc.)
- **Download QR** — Save your store's QR code as an image forprinting or sharing

Customers who scan the QR code go directly to your store page.

---

### 9. Notifications

- The 🔔 bell icon in the top menu shows unread notifications.
- You receive notifications when:
  - A **new order** is placed by a customer
  - An item drops **below minimum stock**
- Click the bell to view all notifications and mark them as read.

---

### 10. Change Password & Username

-**Change Password** — Click **Change Password** in the top menu. Enter your old password, new password, and confirm.
- **Change Username** — Go to **My Profile** → **Change Username**. You can log in with either your username or email.
